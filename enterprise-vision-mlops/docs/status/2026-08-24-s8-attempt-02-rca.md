## Observed Failure

Fresh control, one-second latency, and transient-once profiles passed all nine
repetitions. The first retry-budget repetition accepted 23 tasks and closed all
23 with zero duplicate effects and complete trace identity, but five intended
transient items reached the queue max-age and became `expired` instead of the
required retry-budget `dlq` outcome. The fault gate stopped immediately and no
soak workload started.

## Root Cause

The runner had drifted from its frozen TOML contract: it hard-coded twenty
transient plus two healthy requests instead of reading the declared twelve plus
four. In addition, the initial global retry budget of 32 per 60 seconds was
higher than the dependency circuit's effective half-open throughput. The
circuit therefore serialized failing probes until max-age could win before the
budget was exhausted. The resulting expiry was explicit and safe, but it did
not answer the intended retry-budget/DLQ engineering question.

## Remediation

The v2 profile binds the runner directly to the declared request counts and
freezes the global budget at eight per 60 seconds, below the circuit-limited
attempt rate. The runner now records monotonic fault-to-terminal elapsed time
instead of reusing the final poll duration. Config/runtime mismatch is covered
by focused tests. The full 21-repetition fault matrix must restart on the v2
revision; none of the nine passing results from this attempt can be reused.

## Claim Boundary

This retained attempt proves fail-closed terminal accounting and cleanup for a
rejected local run only. It is not S8 acceptance, production traffic, SLA,
HA/DR, multi-node, multi-GPU, or simultaneous multi-model GPU execution
evidence.
